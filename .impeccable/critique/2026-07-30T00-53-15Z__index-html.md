## Overall Impression

A distinctive, disciplined little site scoring 34/40 with zero P0s. The visual system is genuinely yours: two inks with strict jobs, a misprint signature, working keyboard layer, thought-through degradation (no-JS, print, reduced-motion, clipboard fallback). The problems are all behavioral, and they cluster around one theme: the intro choreography and the conversion action both misfire in ways that specifically hit this site's stated audience — the 30-second recruiter and the view-source engineer.

## What's Working

1. Color discipline: coral never leaks into interactive elements; both themes hold the same identity; dark-mode coral (6.8:1) is better tuned than the light (4.02:1).
2. The keyboard layer: 1/2/3 verified working, ? legend, Escape, badges stripped on mobile — the "engineer who makes things" claim demonstrated, not stated.
3. Degradation depth: no-JS keeps the conversion card and hides the dead-end nav; print forces light ink; reduced-motion lands on finished states; clipboard failure falls back to mailto.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] Intro choreography replays on every return to "hi". display:none resets CSS animations, so the border re-sweeps and the name flattens for 4.1s on every tab round-trip (verified via getAnimations()). Fix: stamp .intro-done after first animationend and pin end states, or hide panels without display:none. Command: polish.
2. [P1] Email conversion action is silent for screen readers and surprising for everyone. Click hijacks mailto for a clipboard write; "copied!" is never announced (no aria-live); SR users perceive nothing happening on the site's one conversion action. Fix: keep the link a true mailto, make the "copy" hint the clipboard target, wrap confirmation in role="status". Command: harden.
3. [P2] ~30KB of unreachable content ships: ~40 cool entries in live DOM behind a dead tab, two full blog posts in comments, ~360 lines of parked dice physics in main.js. Crawlers index the hidden list; view-source engineers find a site contradicting its own nav. Fix: move unpublished content to a branch; strip parked code. Command: distill.
4. [P2] Modifier clicks hijacked: unconditional preventDefault on tab links breaks Cmd/Ctrl+click for the exact power-user audience courted. Fix: bail when metaKey/ctrlKey/shiftKey/non-left button. Command: harden.
5. [P3] The signature peak lands at 4.1s — after the median fold decision. Fix: compress timeline ~2x (sweep ~1.3s, slashes by ~2s, misprint ~2.2s). Command: animate.

## Persona Red Flags

Alex (power user): Cmd+click downgraded to same-tab nav; unskippable intro that replays per tab switch — the classic red flag, twice.
Sam (screen reader): conversion action completes in silence; copied address never spoken; aria-disabled on non-interactive spans is noise. Otherwise strong (skip link, focus-visible, AA contrast both themes).
Casey (mobile): h1 at 40px/1.5px tracking has no ≤600px rule — "Sachdeva" wraps awkwardly at 320-375px; four adjacent contact links at ~24px height; "coming soon" tooltip invisible on touch.

## Minor Observations

- Home card bottom-heavy: contact-line trailing margin stacks with card padding.
- Anthropic Sans 600 used but not preloaded (late swap on music tab); browser also warns the preloaded 400/700 aren't used within seconds of load.
- .affil-row keeps flex+gap styling for the commented-out canvas.
- Direct #music load leaves the title bare; clicking sets "music · Ankit Sachdeva".
- Stale comment: "Keyboard tab shortcuts (1–5)" — map is 1–3.

## Questions to Consider

1. Should the misprint be first-seen rather than last-earned? If fringes were present at t=0 and only the sweep animated, the site loses a bug surface and the signature meets every visitor.
2. What do two struck-through tabs buy that shipping the one finished blog post (already written, sitting in a comment) wouldn't?
3. If a senior engineer you admire opened view-source, is "workshop mid-move" the story you want told? For this audience, source is surface.
